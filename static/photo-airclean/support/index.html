Frequently asked questions

Why does the app need photo access?

Photo AirClean reads media information to calculate file sizes, generate thumbnails, and compress or delete items only when you request those actions. You can change access in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos.

Are my photos and videos uploaded?

No. Media scanning, size analysis, and cleaning run on your device. RevenueCat verifies Pro purchase access, but your photo and video content is not uploaded for that purpose.

Can I recover a deleted item?

Items deleted through Photo AirClean normally move to the Photos app's Recently Deleted album. Recover them there before the system retention period expires.

Does compression preserve metadata?

The app preserves available capture date, location, camera, lens, and other metadata whenever the target media format supports it.

What does Pro unlock?

The free version allows three compression or deletion operations per day. Photo AirClean Pro unlocks unlimited operations and is available as monthly, yearly, or lifetime access.

My scan looks incomplete

Confirm that Photo AirClean has full photo-library access, then return to the Home screen and run a new scan. Large libraries can take longer to analyze.

Contact support

Contact the developer through Telegram support. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, Photo AirClean version, steps to reproduce the issue, and a screenshot that does not reveal private media.

常见问题

为什么需要相册权限？

App 需要读取媒体信息来计算文件大小、生成缩略图，并在你明确操作时压缩或删除项目。你可以在“设置 > 隐私与安全性 > 照片”中修改权限。

照片和视频会上传吗？

不会。媒体扫描、大小分析和清理流程在设备本地完成。RevenueCat 只用于验证 Pro 购买权益，不会因此上传照片和视频内容。

删除后还能恢复吗？

通过 Photo AirClean 删除的媒体通常会进入系统“照片”App 的“最近删除”相册。请在系统规定的保留期限内恢复。

压缩会保留元数据吗？

只要目标媒体格式支持，App 会尽可能保留拍摄日期、位置、相机、镜头和其他可用元数据。

专业版可以解锁什么？

免费用户每天可执行 3 次压缩或删除操作。Photo AirClean Pro 解锁无限次操作，并提供月付、年付和终身买断三种方案。

扫描结果不完整怎么办？

请确认 App 已获得完整照片访问权限，然后返回首页重新扫描。大型媒体库可能需要更长时间完成分析。
