Photo AirClean Privacy Policy

Effective July 26, 2026

English简体中文Support

Photo AirClean respects your privacy. This policy explains how the app handles information while scanning, compressing, organizing, and managing purchase access.

Photos and videos

Photo AirClean requests access to your photo library to calculate media sizes, generate thumbnails, and perform compression or deletion actions that you explicitly choose.

  • Media analysis and cleaning are performed on your device.
  • Your photos and videos are not uploaded to the developer's servers as part of scanning, compression, or deletion.
  • You can change or revoke photo-library access at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Deleted items normally move to the system Recently Deleted album and remain subject to iOS retention rules.

Data stored on your device

The app stores operational data locally, including media scan results and free-use counters. Deleting the app removes this local app data, but does not automatically remove original media or items in the system Recently Deleted album.

In-app purchases

Photo AirClean uses Apple In-App Purchase and RevenueCat to process purchases, verify purchase status, restore transactions, and unlock Pro features. Apple and RevenueCat may process transaction records, an app-scoped user identifier, and technical information such as app, device, and operating-system versions. This information is used to provide purchase functionality and is not used by Photo AirClean for cross-app tracking or third-party advertising.

Learn more in Apple's Privacy Policy and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Tracking and advertising

Photo AirClean does not use your data for cross-app tracking and does not include third-party advertising services.

Retention and your choices

  • Local scan records remain on your device until they are refreshed, cleared, or the app is deleted.
  • Purchase records are retained by Apple and RevenueCat according to their respective policies.
  • You can revoke photo access in iOS Settings and delete the app to remove its local data.

Children's privacy

Photo AirClean does not knowingly collect children's personal information. Photo and video content is processed locally on the device.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when app functionality or data handling changes. The current effective date will be shown on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through Telegram support.

Photo AirClean 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 7 月 26 日

Photo AirClean 重视你的隐私。本政策说明 App 在提供相册扫描、压缩、整理与购买权益功能时如何处理信息。

照片与视频

为了计算媒体大小、生成缩略图，以及执行你明确选择的压缩或删除操作，Photo AirClean 会请求访问设备上的照片与视频。

  • 照片与视频的分析和清理在设备本地完成。
  • Photo AirClean 不会因为扫描、压缩或删除流程而把照片或视频上传到开发者服务器。
  • 你可以随时在 iOS“设置”中修改或撤销相册访问权限。
  • 被删除的项目通常会进入系统“最近删除”相册，并按照 iOS 规则保留一段时间。

设备本地数据

App 会在设备本地保存媒体扫描结果、免费操作次数等运行所需信息。删除 App 会清除这些本地数据，但不会自动清除系统照片图库中的原始媒体或“最近删除”相册中的内容。

App 内购买

Photo AirClean 使用 Apple App 内购买和 RevenueCat 处理购买、验证购买状态、恢复交易并解锁 Pro 功能。Apple 和 RevenueCat 可能处理交易记录、App 范围内的用户标识符，以及 App、设备和操作系统版本等技术信息。这些信息仅用于提供购买功能，Photo AirClean 不会将其用于跨 App 跟踪或第三方广告。

更多信息请参阅 Apple 隐私政策和 RevenueCat 隐私政策。

跟踪与广告

Photo AirClean 不使用用户数据进行跨 App 跟踪，也不集成第三方广告服务。

数据保留与选择

  • 本地扫描记录会保存在设备上，直至数据被刷新、清理或 App 被删除。
  • 购买记录由 Apple 和 RevenueCat 按各自政策处理和保留。
  • 你可以在 iOS“设置”中撤销相册权限，也可以删除 App 以清除其本地数据。

儿童隐私

Photo AirClean 不会有意收集儿童的个人资料。照片与视频内容只在设备本地处理。

政策更新

如果 App 的功能或数据处理方式发生变化，本政策可能会更新，并在本页面标明新的生效日期。

联系我们

如对本隐私政策有疑问，请通过 Telegram 技术支持联系开发者。
