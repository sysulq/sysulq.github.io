Privacy policy

Effective September 11, 2026 · Developer: Qi Li (sysulq)

English简体中文Support

Your bill records stay on your device. Purchase services are separate and can communicate with Apple and RevenueCat.

1. Local bill records

Bill names, amounts, currencies, billing dates, recurrence rules, categories, notes, review acknowledgements, saved exchange rates and preferences are stored locally in a protected iOS App Group shared with the Bill Atlas widget. We do not receive these records. There is no Bill Atlas account, bank connection, email scanning, advertising SDK or automatic cloud sync.

2. Purchases and paywalls

Bill Atlas uses Apple In-App Purchase and RevenueCat/RevenueCatUI to display offers, process and restore purchases, verify Pro access and operate the paywall. When configured, RevenueCat initializes at launch and may contact its service even before a purchase.

Processing may include an automatically generated app-scoped purchase identifier, transaction receipts and purchase history, product and entitlement status, paywall interactions, and technical app/device information for purchase service operation and analytics. An anonymous identifier does not mean no personal data is processed. We do not provide bill records, your name or email as subscriber attributes, collect payment-card details, use advertising identifiers or enable advertising/attribution integrations.

Apple and RevenueCat may process purchase information outside your country under their policies. See Apple Privacy and RevenueCat Privacy. We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-app advertising tracking.

3. Notifications and widgets

Reminders are scheduled on the device, without a remote push server. Widgets read local records. Names and amounts are hidden by default; showing details can expose them on your Home Screen, notifications or StandBy. iOS controls delivery and visibility.

4. Backups, deletion and retention

The private ledger is excluded from automatic device and cloud backups. Export your own backup before replacing or deleting the app. Exports are unencrypted JSON containing your records. A destination you select, including a cloud provider or sharing service, may move the copy off your device under that provider's terms.

Delete individual bills in the app to remove them from the active local ledger. Uninstalling the app can remove local records; it does not remove exported copies or Apple/RevenueCat transaction records. Manage exported files at their destinations. Restoring Pro restores purchase access, not bill data. Apple and RevenueCat retain purchase information according to their policies and applicable requirements.

5. Support and your choices

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to address your request. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction, object to processing, or contact a supervisory authority. We cannot retrieve bill records from a server because we do not receive them. Identifying purchase records may require a purchase reference; never send passwords, full payment details or private bill exports to a public channel.

Bill Atlas is a general personal planning tool. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact us to request deletion. Contact Qi Li through the developer's existing Telegram support channel and identify the request as about Bill Atlas.

6. This website and changes

These pages use no analytics scripts, forms, cookies or third-party fonts. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may log IP addresses for security. See GitHub's Privacy Statement. External links and support platforms have their own policies. We will update this page and its effective date when practices change.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 11 日 · 开发者：Qi Li（sysulq）

1. 本地账单

账单名称、金额、币种、日期、周期、分类、备注、评估标记、汇率和偏好存储在设备受保护的 App Group 中，仅供本 App 与小组件使用。我们不接收这些账单记录。无需注册、连接银行或读取邮件，不含广告 SDK，也不自动云同步。

2. 购买与付费墙

Apple 与 RevenueCat／RevenueCatUI 提供商品展示、购买、恢复购买和 Pro 权益验证。配置后的 RevenueCat 会在启动时初始化，因此购买前也可能联网。处理的信息可能包括自动生成的 App 范围购买标识、收据、交易历史、商品与权益状态、付费墙交互及 App／设备技术信息，用于购买服务和相关分析。匿名标识不等于不处理个人数据。

我们不将账单记录、姓名或邮箱作为订阅者属性发送，不收集银行卡信息，不使用广告标识符或广告归因集成。Apple 与 RevenueCat 可能依其政策跨境处理购买信息，详见上方对应隐私政策。我们不出售个人信息，也不进行跨 App 广告跟踪。

3. 提醒与小组件

提醒在设备本地安排，无远程推送服务器。小组件读取本地记录，默认隐藏名称和金额。主动显示详情可能使信息出现在主屏幕、通知或待机显示中；呈现及送达由 iOS 控制。

4. 备份、删除与保留

私有账本不纳入自动设备或云备份。更换设备或删除 App 前请自行导出。导出的 JSON 未加密，包含账单记录；选择云端或共享目的地可能使副本离开设备，适用该服务的政策。

可在 App 中删除单笔账单。卸载可能移除本地数据，但不会删除导出副本或 Apple／RevenueCat 的交易记录。请在保存目的地管理副本。恢复 Pro 不会恢复账单。购买服务依据各自政策及适用要求保留交易信息。

5. 支持与权利

主动联系支持时，我们接收你选择提供的信息，用于处理请求。依据适用法律，你可能享有访问、更正、删除、限制处理、提出异议或向监管机构投诉的权利。由于我们不接收账单，无法从服务器取回它们。定位交易可能需要购买参考信息，请勿在公开频道发送密码、完整支付信息或账单导出文件。若儿童向支持渠道发送了个人信息，可联系请求删除。

请通过开发者现有的 Telegram 支持渠道联系 Qi Li，并注明 Bill Atlas。

6. 网站与更新

这些页面不含分析脚本、表单、Cookie 或第三方字体。GitHub Pages 提供托管，可能因安全需要记录 IP，适用 GitHub 隐私政策。外部链接与支持平台有各自政策。数据实践变化时，我们会更新页面和生效日期。
