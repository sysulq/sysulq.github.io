Getting started

Add a bill with its original amount, currency, next billing date and recurrence. Forecasts show estimated upcoming charges, not confirmed bank transactions. Reviewing or archiving a bill never cancels a merchant subscription.

Reminders

Allow notifications in iOS Settings and choose 1, 2 or 3 days before a bill in its editor. Check scheduled coverage in Bill Atlas Settings and reopen the app regularly to extend it. Focus, device settings and iOS affect delivery. Reviewing a charge silences that occurrence's reminder; undo the review to restore it while scheduling time remains.

Exchange rates

Rates are a dated reference snapshot, not live quotes. Choose a display currency or edit rates in Settings. Bills retain their original currency. Estimates may differ from a bank's final exchange rate or fees.

Widgets and StandBy

Touch and hold the Home Screen, add a Bill Atlas widget, and use its checkmark to mark the displayed charge reviewed. On supported iPhones, enable StandBy and charge the locked phone on its side. Add Bill Atlas to a StandBy widget stack and pair it with Apple's clock. iOS controls whether the screen stays on.

Protect your records

Export an unencrypted JSON backup from Settings to a place you control. Bill records are excluded from automatic backups. Import previews a merge, adding missing records and preserving existing matching records. Uninstalling or losing your device may lose records without an exported backup.

Pro and restoring purchases

Three active bills and essential features are free. Pro is a one-time purchase for unlimited active bills and alternate icons. Use Restore purchases with the Apple Account used to buy Pro. An internet connection is needed to buy or restore; verified cached Pro access can work offline. Restoring purchases does not restore bills. If an entitlement is revoked, existing bills remain readable, editable and exportable.

Contact

Contact Qi Li (sysulq) through Telegram support, identifying Bill Atlas. Include the app version, iPhone model, iOS version and steps to reproduce. Redact bill names, amounts and identifiers in screenshots. Do not post sensitive records or payment details in public.

使用支持

开始使用

手动填写原始金额、币种、扣费日期与周期。预测是预计扣费，不是银行实付记录。评估或归档账单都不会取消商家的订阅。

提醒没有收到

请在 iOS 设置中允许通知，在账单编辑页选择提前 1–3 天。查看 App 设置中的已安排覆盖范围，并定期打开 App 延长提醒计划。专注模式和系统设置可能影响送达。标记已评估会静默该次提醒；仍在可安排时间内可撤销评估。

汇率与预测

内置汇率是带日期的离线参考，可手动修改，并非实时或银行结算汇率。切换显示币种不改变账单原币种，预测可能与实付和手续费不同。

小组件和待机显示

长按主屏幕添加 Bill Atlas 小组件。勾选按钮只标记当前扣费已评估。支持的 iPhone 可开启待机显示，锁屏横放充电，并在待机组件叠放中添加 Bill Atlas，与系统时钟搭配。屏幕是否保持显示由 iOS 决定。

备份和恢复

私有账本不纳入自动备份，请在设置中主动导出未加密 JSON 到你控制的位置。导入会先预览合并，添加缺失记录并保留相同标识的已有记录。没有导出备份时，卸载或设备丢失可能导致数据丢失。

Pro 购买

免费管理 3 笔活跃账单。Pro 一次购买解锁无限活跃账单与替换图标。请使用原购买 Apple 账户恢复购买；购买和恢复需要联网，已验证的缓存权益可离线使用。恢复 Pro 不会恢复账单。权益撤销后，已有账单仍可读取、编辑和导出。
