STILL · MINDFUL SCREEN TIME

Privacy Policy

Effective September 10, 2026 · Developer: Qi Li (sysulq)

English简体中文Contact

Your selected apps and pause decisions stay on your iPhone. Still has no app account, advertising SDK or custom activity-data backend. Apple and RevenueCat handle purchases, so Still is not a “no data collected” app.

1. Screen Time and local information

Still requests individual Screen Time authorization when you choose to set up protection. Apple's Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity frameworks provide opaque tokens for the apps you select. Still uses these tokens to show shields, run your pause workflow, temporarily allow one app and restore protection. A category selection saves the current individual app tokens rather than a list sent to a server.

The app and its extensions share local settings and records through an iOS App Group. These include selected-app tokens, protection and automation settings, pending pauses, temporary access grants, appearance and haptic preferences, and dated pause decisions and measured foreground pause time. Still does not read what you view, type or post inside another app. “Mindful time” measures pauses; it is not health data inferred from your behavior or an estimate of time saved.

Still does not send app selections, Screen Time tokens, browsing content or pause records to the developer or RevenueCat. Widgets and Shortcuts use this local state. Your iPhone's backup settings may include app data; device and backup services are governed by Apple's settings and policies.

2. Purchases and RevenueCat

Still uses Apple In-App Purchase and RevenueCat/RevenueCatUI to show offers, process and restore purchases, verify Pro access and operate the paywall. RevenueCat is initialized when the configured app launches and may communicate with its service before you buy anything.

Purchase processing can involve an automatically generated app-scoped purchase identifier, receipts and transaction history, product and entitlement status, and technical app/device information. RevenueCat also processes purchase and paywall-related information for service operation and purchase analytics. An anonymous purchase identifier is not the same as an absence of personal data. Still does not provide your name or email as subscriber attributes, collect your payment-card details, use advertising identifiers, or enable advertising/attribution integrations.

Apple and RevenueCat may process information outside your country under their respective practices and safeguards. Read Apple's Privacy Policy and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy. Still does not sell your information or use it for cross-app advertising tracking.

3. Retention and your controls

  • Still keeps a rolling 30-day pause history; older records are pruned when the app updates or records activity. Settings and selected-app tokens remain until you change or reset them.
  • In Settings, “Erase pause history” removes recorded decisions and pause totals while keeping protection. “Reset local data” clears local selection, history and settings and turns protection off; verified purchase access may be retained or restored.
  • You can turn protection off in Still and revoke Screen Time access in iOS Settings. Reauthorization may require choosing your apps again.
  • Resetting or deleting Still does not delete Apple or RevenueCat transaction records, remove device backups, or cancel a purchase. Those providers retain records under their policies and applicable requirements.

4. Privacy requests and support

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of processing, or to object or complain to a supervisory authority. Contact the developer below for assistance. We cannot retrieve Screen Time selections or pause history from a server because we do not receive them. Identifying a purchase record may require additional purchase-reference information; please do not send passwords, full payment details, Screen Time tokens or private app activity.

If you voluntarily contact support, we receive the messages and information you choose to share and use them to answer your request. The support platform handles messages under its own policies. Still is a personal attention tool, not a service for remotely monitoring children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact us to request its removal.

5. This website

This privacy page contains no analytics scripts, cookies, forms or third-party fonts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes; that hosting activity is separate from Still's on-device data. See GitHub Pages information and the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following external links opens services with their own policies.

6. Changes

We may update this policy when Still's features or data practices change. The effective date and current policy will appear on this page.

Still 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 10 日 · 开发者：Qi Li（sysulq）

你选择的 App 与暂停记录保存在 iPhone 本地。Still 无需注册 App 账号，不含广告 SDK，也没有上传使用行为的自建后端。购买功能由 Apple 和 RevenueCat 提供，因此不能将 Still 描述为“完全不收集数据”。

1. 屏幕使用时间与本地信息

当你主动设置保护时，Still 会请求个人屏幕使用时间权限。Apple 的 Family Controls、Managed Settings 和 Device Activity 框架提供所选 App 的不透明标识。Still 用它们显示拦截页、执行暂停流程、临时放行一个 App 并恢复保护。整类选择会保存当前各个 App 的标识，而不是将类别或 App 列表发送到服务器。

主 App 与扩展通过 iOS App Group 共享本地设置和记录，包括所选 App 标识、保护与自动化设置、未完成的暂停、临时访问许可、外观与震动偏好，以及带时间的暂停选择和实际前台暂停时长。Still 不读取你在其他 App 中浏览、输入或发布的内容。“清醒时长”仅计算暂停时间，不推断健康状况，也不估算节省时间。

Still 不向开发者或 RevenueCat 发送 App 选择、屏幕使用时间标识、浏览内容或暂停记录。小组件和快捷指令使用上述本地状态。设备备份可能包含 App 数据，具体取决于你的 iPhone 设置；设备与备份服务适用 Apple 的设置和政策。

2. 购买与 RevenueCat

Still 使用 Apple App 内购买及 RevenueCat／RevenueCatUI 展示商品、处理和恢复购买、验证 Pro 权益并运行付费墙。配置完成的 App 启动时就会初始化 RevenueCat，因此即使尚未购买，也可能与其服务通信。

购买流程可能涉及自动生成的 App 范围内购买标识、收据和交易历史、商品与权益状态，以及 App／设备技术信息。RevenueCat 也会为服务运行和购买分析处理购买及付费墙相关信息。匿名购买标识不意味着不存在个人数据。Still 不将你的姓名或邮箱作为订阅者属性提供，不收集银行卡信息，不使用广告标识符，也不启用广告或归因集成。

Apple 和 RevenueCat 可能依据各自的数据处理措施，在你所在国家或地区以外处理信息。详情请参阅 Apple 隐私政策与 RevenueCat 隐私政策。Still 不出售你的信息，也不将其用于跨 App 广告跟踪。

3. 保留期限与自主控制

  • Still 保留滚动 30 天的暂停历史，并在更新状态或记录活动时清理过期记录。设置和所选 App 标识会保留至你修改或重置。
  • 设置中的“清除暂停记录”会清除选择记录和暂停统计，但保留保护。“重置本地数据”会清除本地选择、历史与设置并关闭保护；已验证的购买权益可能被保留或恢复。
  • 你可以在 Still 中关闭保护，或在 iOS 设置中撤销屏幕使用时间权限。重新授权后可能需要重新选择 App。
  • 重置或删除 Still 不会删除 Apple 或 RevenueCat 的交易记录，不会删除设备备份，也不会取消购买。相关提供方按各自政策及适用要求保留记录。

4. 隐私请求与支持

根据适用法律，你可能享有访问、更正、删除、限制处理、提出异议或向监管机构投诉等权利。请通过下方联系方式联系开发者。由于我们不接收屏幕使用时间选择与暂停历史，无法从服务器取回这些数据。定位购买记录可能需要额外的购买参考信息；请勿发送密码、完整支付信息、屏幕使用时间标识或私人 App 活动。

如果你主动联系支持，我们会收到你选择分享的消息和资料，并用于回复请求；支持平台按其自身政策处理消息。Still 是个人注意力工具，并非远程监控儿童的服务。如你认为儿童向支持渠道发送了个人信息，可联系我们请求删除。

5. 本网页

本隐私页面不含分析脚本、Cookie、表单或第三方字体，由 GitHub Pages 托管。GitHub 会出于安全目的记录访问者 IP 地址，这与 Still 的本地设备数据相互独立。请参阅 GitHub Pages 说明和 GitHub 隐私声明。外部链接指向的服务适用其各自政策。

6. 政策更新

Still 的功能或数据处理方式变化时，我们可能更新本政策。本页会公布当前版本及生效日期。
