# sysulq.github.io

Simple blog for me.

Product privacy pages are standalone HTML under `static/` and are copied by Hugo without theme scripts.

- Still: [`static/still/privacy/index.html`](static/still/privacy/index.html), published at <https://sysulq.github.io/still/privacy/>.

Pushes to `main` deploy through `.github/workflows/hugo.yml`. Check the deployment and public URL after updating a policy; a local commit alone does not publish it.

- Bill Atlas: `static/bill-atlas/` — product, support and privacy pages at <https://sysulq.github.io/bill-atlas/>. Standalone HTML without analytics or theme scripts.
